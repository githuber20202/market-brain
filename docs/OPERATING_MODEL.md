# Operating Model

1. Overnight, the Slow Brain refreshes quality and catalyst Evidence Cards.
2. The market stream continuously discovers and re-ranks symbols.
3. A deterministic plan is issued only when structure exists.
4. A fresh authoritative market event activates the plan.
5. The Risk Wallet reserves capacity and emits BUY_NOW with exact quantity.
6. The user executes manually and confirms the actual fill.
7. The Position Twin becomes the source of truth for that trade.
8. Live market events produce HOLD, TRIM, TAKE_PROFIT or SELL_NOW.
9. The user confirms exits, releasing capital in the Risk Wallet.
10. Shadow outcomes and real outcomes are reviewed separately.
